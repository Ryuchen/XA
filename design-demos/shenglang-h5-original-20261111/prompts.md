每张图使用下方通用说明，再追加对应页面指令。

原创高保真中文移动 H5 界面设计稿，项目「声浪电竞」，艺术方向「母带 MASTER」。A real Chinese mobile product UI, not artwork for a pitch. Deliver a single extremely sharp front-facing full-bleed phone UI, portrait 960×2080 or similar, logically 390×844 CSS px. No surrounding device, slide, annotations, phones or outside margins. If more content essential, use a tall scroll capture with a single ending control. Typography: highly legible MiSans Simplified Chinese and sharply condensed numerals. Do not misdraw characters. Tone: Japanese electronic record-sleeve art direction meets modern iOS editorial usability. Signature graphic: vibrating speaker membrane / oscillogram printed in blue halftone, concentric grooves cut into ink-black vinyl, alternating huge flat editorial type and quiet areas of warm uncoated paper. Original monochrome documentary imagery of adult gamers when relevant, hard studio flash, cobalt spot-color screen print, precise registration and deliberately asymmetric crops. Palette print black #101116, bone #F6F3EA, vivid cobalt #204DFF, soft gray rules. Art always subordinate to tasks. Avoid prior interface conventions: absolutely no translucent blue waves, glassmorphism, stock pill dashboard, game mascot, rainbow neon or repeated big banner. Treat homepage as a record sleeve, transactions as legible receipts, accounts as a pass, roster as a talent contact sheet; background should be smooth and quiet around body copy. Brand stamp is tight typographic “声浪” with one small parallel-groove mark. Beautiful Chinese typography, clear primary action, task-first hierarchy, 20px interactive gutters, generous tap height. Use only necessary user-facing words, no decorative issue numbers, no phantom services, no extra English slogans or UI headings. All mobile nav text must exactly follow specified page mode. ONLY print the simplified Chinese content of the page, never any instructions; where values needed, use fictional 声浪电竞, customer 小屿, SL-阿澈, 王者双排, order SL-031, 48 浪花, available 268 浪花, 360 积分 as appropriate. Retain store domain boundary and distinguish reference prices from payment.
